# CONTEXT — Mashwara glossary

Domain glossary for this codebase. Code, tests, ADRs and conversation use these words
with these meanings. Add a term here in the same commit that introduces it.

---

## People

**Patient.** The person at the counter. Speaks Urdu (Hindi fallback, ADR 0003). Never
touches the screen; hears the app in her language. Our demo patient is **Nani**.

**Counter operator.** Whoever holds the tablet behind the counter — the shopkeeper or a
**volunteer**. Speaks English. Reads the live transcript, asks follow-ups out loud, holds
the sale when a flag appears, sends the case. Types nothing.

**Volunteer.** A counter operator who is giving time rather than working the shop. Same
screen, same role. One of the two giving mechanisms in the philanthropic claim.

**Pharmacist.** A qualified, remote, pro bono pharmacist. Opens cases from a queue,
reviews, writes advice, approves. Asynchronous — minutes to hours (ADR 0004). The other
giving mechanism.

## The case

**Patient file** (`PatientFile`). One patient visit, in the shape a doctor expects.
Opened with three fields (name, age, sex); closed when the pharmacist signs. Shape in
`docs/specs/contracts.md`. Lives in the Neon `files` table (ADR 0005). Starts from zero —
no chart (ADR 0002) — but she may bring documents.

**Recording.** A monologue: she talks, uninterrupted, into a mic the app opened for her
alone. Single-speaker by construction (ADR 0007). Recording 1 is her whole account.

**History.** Recording 1 as one block, Urdu and English.

**Live translate.** The two-language interpreter screen used after Recording 1. One
screen, two widths (phone P3, web W0). Each **turn** is attributed by the language heard
— Urdu is hers, English is yours (ADR 0007).

**Ask her this.** One open question at a time on the phone; tap to have it spoken in Urdu,
or read it yourself. The answer lands in live translate and closes the question.

**Attachment.** A prescription, report or strip photo she brings. Gemini extracts
`MedItem`s with `source: "document"`. A document never overwrites her voice; disagreement
raises a question.

**Report.** The signed file rendered for a doctor: presenting request, how it was taken,
history, medicines, interactions, questions asked and unanswered, impression, advice,
**limitations** (always present).

**Med item.** One thing the patient is asking for or already takes. Always carries a
**term** (from the closed vocabulary), **her words** (the exact original-language phrase
that produced it), a **source** (`voice` or `photo`) and a **role**.

**Role.** `requested` — what she came to buy. `takes` — a medicine she already uses.
`remedy` — a home/herbal/hakeem remedy. `prescribed` — from a document, not her account.
Role tags are never colored.

**Source.** `voice` | `photo` | `document`. Voice and photo items must carry her words;
a document item has none and the row says so.

**Provenance pair.** The clinical term and her words, rendered together, always. A UI
invariant: a med item never appears without the words that produced it.

**Unidentified.** (was: unrecognised) Gemini could not map her words to a vocabulary term. Not an error —
it sends the app back to ask a follow-up. Renders as a dashed card.

## Safety vocabulary

**Closed vocabulary.** ~150 terms: common Pakistani OTC brands and generics, chronic
meds, and common desi remedies. Gemini may only output a term from this list or
`unrecognised`. Owned by stream A. File: `data/substances.json` (`substances[]`).

**Interaction table.** ~40 rows of `{ termA, termB, severity, reason, source }`. The only
thing that can produce a flag (ADR 0001). Every row has a citable source. File:
`data/substances.json` (`interactions[]`).

**Flag.** A row from the interaction table that matches two med items in the file.
Carries severity (`high` | `moderate`), the reason and a **citation**. A flag without a
citation cannot render. Never generated by an LLM.

**Question.** Anything the model suspects, phrased as a question in Urdu and English,
with **why** it was raised and **from** where in the recording. Rendered interrogative,
in the `ask` color, with a `?` glyph. Never a statement, never a flag (ADR 0001). Asked
on the phone one at a time; answered in live translate.

**Hold.** Nothing is sold before the pharmacist signs. The physical beat of the demo.

**Severity.** `high` or `moderate`. Sets bubble size on the map and the label on a flag.

## Advice

**Advice.** The pharmacist's plan in Urdu and English plus a per-item **verdict**, signed
with name, qualification and registration. **Impression** is the pharmacist's one-line
clinical read, on the report only.

**Verdict.** `keep` | `stop` | `swap`, one per med item. `stop` is the only colored chip.

**Readback.** The advice spoken to the patient in her language via TTS. Step 10.

## Surfaces

**Phone** (P1–P5): new case, let her talk, live translate, ask her this, advice in Urdu.
The counter operator holds it; she reads it at arm's length. Nothing under 44px.

**Web app** (W0–W4): live translate at web width, the file as it builds, findings and
what to ask, importing a document, the report. The counter operator's desk view.

**Pharmacist console** (2A–2C): queue, file open, signed and sent.

**Status.** `new → recording → structured → asking → sent → signed`. Forward only.

**Bubble map.** Nodes = med items; size = worst severity touching the node; fill = status
color or neutral; edges = interaction rows. Hover shows her words.

## Design words

**Status tokens.** `clear`, `ask`, `flag`. The only saturated colors in the product.
"The only color on screen is a flag" (ADR 0006).

**Nastaliq.** Noto Nastaliq Urdu, the typeface for every patient-facing string. RTL,
line-height ~1.9. Urdu never falls back to a Latin face.
