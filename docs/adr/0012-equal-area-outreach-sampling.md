# 0012 — Shaded outreach sampling areas

Status: Accepted for Pasni pilot
Date: 2026-09-20
Supersedes: ADR 0011 measurement geometry (all other constraints retained)

The owner requested colored areas rather than radius outlines and approved the label Potential access gaps. Six geodesic hexagons centered on the existing GeoNames settlements replace 5 km circles. Each has a 2 km center-to-vertex distance and approximately 10.4 square kilometres of area. These are equal-size sampling cells, not official village or district boundaries. They do not establish service catchments.

The same closed counterclockwise polygon function drives Google Places Aggregate customArea requests and Google Maps rendering. Counts are recomputed inside those exact shapes. Never reuse radius-based counts. Colors encode zero matching listings (red), one or two (orange), three or more (teal), or unknown (gray). This is a listing-availability signal for local investigation, not verified clinical or volunteer need. No population-adjusted metric or nationwide extrapolation is claimed.

Selected-area counts, retrieval timestamps, source and limitations are accessible in expandable details. Local-only planning semantics remain available under How to read this map. User-requested marketing copy is removed. Display headings now use Bricolage Grotesque 800, supporting text Figtree, Urdu Nastaliq. Requested liquid-glass surfaces retain crisp content.
